# Read your first meter

Voltway takes a reading from every meter on a site and stores it with the time
it was taken.

1. Add the site in the console.
2. Register the meter serial number.
3. Choose the billing period the readings belong to.

A reading that arrives after the period closes keeps the time it was measured,
so a late delivery never moves a charge into the wrong month.

Each smart meter reports once every fifteen minutes.
