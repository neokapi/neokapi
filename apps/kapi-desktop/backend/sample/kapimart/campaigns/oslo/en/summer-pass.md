# Your summer, sorted

The KapiMart Summer Pass is free for readers aged 13 to 17 in Oslo. Show the
pass at checkout and collect your order from any KapiMart pick-up point in the
city the next day.

## How it works

1. Ask a parent or guardian to add the pass to the family account.
2. Choose a pick-up point near your school, your training or your summer job.
3. Collect your order with the code we send to the family account.

The pass runs until the end of August. Orders you collect from a pick-up point
ship free while it lasts.

## Questions

Your parent or guardian can see every order placed with the pass and can pause
it at any time from the family account.
