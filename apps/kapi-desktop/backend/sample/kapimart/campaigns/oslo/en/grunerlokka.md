# Pick up your order at Grünerløkka

Our newest pick-up point is on Thorvald Meyers gate, a short walk from
Birkelunden. It is open from 10:00 to 20:00 on weekdays and from 10:00 to 18:00
on Saturdays.

Bring the collection code from the family account. If a friend collects for
you, forward the code to them; they do not need a Summer Pass of their own.
