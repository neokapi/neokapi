# Harbor Help fixture facts

Harbor Help is a fictional service used to demonstrate scoped content guidance.
These statements are authored fixture requirements. The deterministic checker
does not establish their truth or find arbitrary contradictions.

## Recording and control

Appointments are not recorded. Screen sharing requires consent and can be
stopped. The adviser cannot control the device or open its files.

## Assurances

Do not describe the service as guaranteed, always safe or risk-free. The sample
enforces a narrow literal pattern for `guarantee`, `always safe` and `risk-free`.
Other unsupported claims still require review.

## Fixture exception

The fixture author allows quoting the prohibited wording in a `policy-example`
channel. The approval fields are asserted local provenance, with no authenticated
review event. This exception applies to none of the audience pages.

## Reading situations

- Child: reads the page with a trusted adult and needs concrete next steps.
- Teen: wants direct instructions and clear privacy information.
- Adult: wants a concise explanation and practical next steps.
- Older adult: is unfamiliar with video appointments and wants unfamiliar steps
  explained. Age alone says nothing about a person's ability.
