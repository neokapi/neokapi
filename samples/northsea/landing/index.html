The port day, in one view

Compass holds the berth plan your duty officer works from. Tidewatch watches the water against it. Together they tell you what is about to go wrong, while there is still time to move a vessel.

Compass — the berth plan, alive

Every arrival is on one plan, and every change to that plan is recorded with the person who made it. Length, draught, and standing restrictions are checked before an assignment is offered, so a berth that cannot take a vessel is never on the list.

Compass runs in a browser and on the quay. It works with the systems you already run: our seamless integration with your port management system means the arrivals board and the plan never disagree.

Tidewatch — warning, not weather

Tidewatch does not forecast the weather. It compares the forecast with the constraints of the berth a vessel is planned into, and raises an alert when a movement stops being safe. Each alert carries the reading that triggered it and the threshold it crossed.

An alert reaches the duty officer on watch and everyone subscribed to the vessel. If nobody acknowledges it, the terminal supervisor hears about it next.

In service
